About

I’m a product designer who writes the code for my own ideas. I like the part of a product most people never notice until it’s missing: how a bubble grows, how a list springs back, how long a button waits before it answers.

Most of my pieces start from a feeling everyone has had on their phone, like waiting for a reply or deleting a message too late, and end as a working interaction you can actually touch.

Product designFlows, screens and systems for apps on phone and desktop.
Interaction and motionGestures, springs, timing and sound that make an interface feel physical.
